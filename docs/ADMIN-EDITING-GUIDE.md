# ShininChrist Website: Editing Guide

For the ministry team. This guide explains what you can change yourself, step by step, and what still needs a developer.

## Getting in

1. Go to the website and log in with your admin account.
2. Open the **Admin Portal** at `/admin`. It shows one card for each area you can manage.
3. Click a card, make your change, then press the green **Save** button on that section.

Saved changes appear on the public website straight away. Allow a few minutes if you do not see a change at once, then refresh the page.

**Who can do what**
- **Admin:** can edit all the content and review members, orders, payments and submissions.
- **Super Admin:** can do everything an Admin can, and can also give or remove roles and suspend a member.

## What you can edit now

### 1. Daily (Admin Portal → Daily Content)
- Change the six Daily activities: title, short line, text, scripture quote, motto, photo, video link and button. Move them up or down, remove them, or add more (up to 12).
- Change the Watch and Listen buttons on the right side of the Today page: YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Audio. Each can be switched on or off, and each has its own address.
- Add dated entries with a devotional, reflection, assignment, photo, video and link. An entry dated this week shows under **This Week**. An older date goes to the **Archive**. A future date stays hidden until that day.
- **Note:** until you save your first real entries, members see the sample entries that came with the site. After your first save, only your own entries show.

### 2. Academy courses (Admin Portal → Academy Courses)
- Create, edit and delete courses. Each course has a title, field of learning, level, duration, short description, full description and a photo.
- Mark each course as **Published** (open), **Coming soon** (listed, not open) or **Draft** (hidden from the public).
- Inside each course, add, edit, reorder and remove **lessons** (text and video), **learning resources** (links) and **assignments** (title, instructions, due note).
- Everyone sees a course overview and its lesson titles. Lesson text, resources and assignments are for active members.
- Students can submit their assignment answers. You read them in **Form Submissions** (see below).

### 3. Site content (Admin Portal → Site Content & Announcements)
- **Homepage hero:** heading, tagline, description, buttons, intro video, and an optional announcement banner at the top of the site.
- **Invite Mercy page:** the top photo and text.
- **FAQs:** add, edit, reorder and remove questions on the FAQ page.

### 4. Header menu and footer (also in Site Content)
- **Header menu:** change the labels and links of the main menu and its dropdowns. The icons and short descriptions in the dropdowns stay as designed.
- **Footer:** change the link columns (Explore, Get Involved, Support, Legal), the contact email and WhatsApp number, and switch the social links (Instagram, TikTok and others) on or off.
- A link can be a page on the site (`/serve`), a full web address (`https://…`), an email (`mailto:`) or a phone number (`tel:`).

### 5. Library (Admin Portal → Digital Library Resources)
- Add, edit and remove library resources: title, description, cover and file.
- Set a resource as a free download, or "give what you can" with an optional amount, including zero.

### 6. Prices and delivery (Admin Portal → Regional Pricing & Delivery)
- Set the uniform price and delivery cost for each country and currency, and switch a country's availability on or off.

### 7. Running the ministry
These are not website content, but you manage them in the same portal:
- **Members & Chapters:** review applications, approve members, assign a chapter, and (Super Admin) change roles or suspend someone.
- **Uniform Orders:** move orders through packing, shipping and delivery.
- **Payments & Proof Review:** check bank-transfer receipts and card payments, then accept or reject.
- **Form Submissions:** all forms in one inbox (Invite Mercy, volunteer, partner, contact, and student assignments). Set a status and add private notes.
- **Designated Bank Accounts:** the bank details shown for transfers.
- **Session Control:** see and end signed-in sessions.

## Photos
- When a form has an **Add photo** button, use a JPG, PNG or WebP file under 4 MB. A clear, landscape photo about 1600 pixels wide works well.
- Videos are added as links. A YouTube link plays on the page. Links from other sites open in a new tab.

## What still needs a developer
These parts of the site are not yet editable from the Admin Portal. For now, a change here means a developer edits the site and redeploys it:
- The text and photos on the **Chapters** pages (Men, Women, Youth), the **Serve** pages, the **About and Founder** pages, **Media and Press**, and the **Give** page.
- The **mission statement** and other homepage text outside the hero.
- Hero photographs on most pages. Photos you upload in the editors above (Invite Mercy, Daily, Courses) you can change yourself.
- **Store** products and prices.
- The **QR codes**.

## Costs and limits
- Everything in "What you can edit now" is included. It does not need a developer or any extra subscription.
- Photos are limited to 4 MB each, and a course can have up to 100 lessons, 100 resources and 100 assignments.
- The email address hello@shininchrist.com needs a mail service (such as Google Workspace or Zoho) before it can receive mail. That is a separate subscription and the domain's mail records must be set up.
- Any change to the pages listed under "What still needs a developer" will be quoted separately unless it is part of Milestone 2 or 3.

## Quick checklist for any change
1. Open the Admin Portal and choose the card.
2. Edit the text, link or photo.
3. Press **Save** and wait for the "Saved" message.
4. Open the public page in a new tab and refresh to check it.
5. If something looks wrong, edit it again and Save. Nothing is lost by saving again.
